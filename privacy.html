Privacy Policy

Bible Verses App

Last Updated: March 14, 2026

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Bible Verses, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our app.

Simple Summary: We don't collect, store, or share your personal information. Your devotional journey is private and belongs to you alone.

Information We DO NOT Collect

Bible Verses is designed with privacy at its core. We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Search history or reading preferences
  • Any data that could identify you personally

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device and never transmitted to our servers:

  • Saved Verses: Verses you bookmark or mark as favorites
  • App Preferences: Your settings and customization choices
  • Reading History: Verses you've recently viewed (stored locally only)

This data remains on your device and is never shared with us or any third party. If you delete the app, this information is permanently removed from your device.

Third-Party Services

Bible Verses does not use any third-party advertising, or tracking services. We do not integrate with social media platforms or share any data with external advertisers.

Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Since we do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Data Security

Because we don't collect or transmit your data, there's no risk of your information being accessed, lost, or misused. All your preferences and saved verses remain securely on your device.

Sharing of Verses

Our app includes a feature that allows you to share Bible verses with others via your device's native sharing options (text message, email, social media, etc.). When you use this feature:

  • The verse text is shared through your chosen platform
  • We do not track or record what you share
  • Any data sharing is handled directly by the platform you select (iMessage, WhatsApp, etc.)

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

If we make any material changes to how we handle data, we will notify you through the app or via other appropriate means.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us:

Email: support.dailybibleverses@protonmail.com

Your Rights

Since we don't collect any personal data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete from our servers. All your app data is stored locally on your device and can be removed by simply deleting the app.
